How It Works

From address to complete measurements

A simple five-step workflow designed for roofing contractors, public adjusters and restoration teams that need measurements without slowing down field operations.

1

Send the Address

Submit the property address, service type and job notes.

2

We Review Imagery

Available overhead and oblique imagery is checked for usable roof detail.

3

We Hand Trace

Roof faces, ridges, hips, valleys, rakes and eaves are traced manually.

4

We Calculate & QA

Areas, pitches, linear measurements and waste tables are checked.

5

You Receive the Report

Receive the requested ESX, XML and/or detailed PDF report.

What You Receive

Measurements built for estimating

  • Ridge, hip, valley, rake and eave lengths

FAQ

Common questions

How do aerial roof measurements work?

You send the property address. We review available overhead and oblique imagery, trace the roof geometry manually, calculate the roof dimensions and deliver the requested report format.

Do I need to visit the property?

No site visit is required when suitable imagery is available. If imagery is not sufficient for reliable tracing, we will let you know.

Which file formats are available?

TrueTrace can deliver Xactimate-ready ESX files, XML files for Symbility-style workflows, and detailed PDF reports depending on the order.
