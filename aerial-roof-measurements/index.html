How It Works
From property address to usable measurements

A simple remote workflow built to reduce field measuring and keep estimating teams moving.

📍

1. Send the Address

Submit the property, service type, format and job notes.

🛰️

2. Review Imagery

Available overhead and oblique imagery is reviewed for roof geometry.

📐

3. Hand Trace

Roof faces and line items are measured and checked by a person.

📁

4. Deliver

Receive ESX, XML and/or a detailed PDF based on your order.

Why aerial measurements make sense

Useful when your team needs measurements quickly without sending someone to every property.

  • Reduce time spent on field measuring
  • Support storm-volume and remote estimating workflows
  • Consistent measurement format across jobs
  • Suitable for simple and complex roof shapes where imagery is usable
What You Receive
Measurements built for estimating
  • Total roof area and squares
  • Ridge, hip, valley, rake and eave lengths
  • Pitch / slope and facet information
  • Roof sketch and annotations where applicable
  • Waste factor table
  • ESX, XML and PDF options
