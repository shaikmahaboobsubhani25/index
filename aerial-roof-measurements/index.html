Professional aerial roof measurements for estimating

TrueTrace measures roofs from aerial imagery and converts the roof geometry into practical estimating deliverables. The service is designed for professionals who need dimensions, pitches, facet areas and edge lengths without spending field time measuring the property.

Best fit: roofing contractors, public adjusters, restoration companies and insurance professionals handling residential, commercial or storm-related work.

What is included

  • Total roof area and facet breakdowns
  • Pitch / slope values where available and measurable
  • Ridge, hip, valley, rake and eave lengths
  • Roof sketch / geometry
  • ESX for Xactimate or XML for Symbility / AppliCad
  • Optional formatted PDF report

Aerial measurement vs automated roof tools

TrueTrace uses a human-traced workflow. That makes the service useful when roof geometry is irregular, the project has multiple structures, or you want a reviewed deliverable rather than an unchecked automated result.
