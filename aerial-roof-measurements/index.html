FAQ
Aerial roof measurement questions
What is an aerial roof measurement?

It is a remote roof measurement prepared from available aerial or satellite imagery rather than an on-site tape measurement.

Can I order ESX or XML?

Yes. TrueTrace offers ESX and XML file options, with a PDF report available where applicable.

What properties do you cover?

Residential, commercial and multifamily properties can be submitted, subject to available imagery and the selected service.

How fast is delivery?

Standard turnaround is stated as 1 business day or less.
