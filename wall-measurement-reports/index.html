FAQ

Common wall-report questions

What is included in a wall measurement report?

The sample report includes wall area details by elevation, front/right/back/left wall views, total wall area, masonry and siding breakdowns, doors and windows, waste-factor calculations and soffit calculations.

Do you provide both residential and commercial wall reports?

Yes. TrueTrace offers residential and commercial wall measurement services.

Can roof and wall measurements be combined?

Yes. The sample provided is a combined roof + wall report, demonstrating how roof and wall information can be included together when the ordered scope requires it.
