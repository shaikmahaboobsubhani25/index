Xactimate and ESX questions
What is an ESX roof report?

An ESX roof measurement deliverable is intended to support an Xactimate-based workflow by providing measurement information in the ESX file format.

Is a PDF included automatically?

The selected package determines whether a PDF is included; the homepage pricing shows file-only and PDF options.

Can I use the service for commercial roofs?

Yes. TrueTrace has commercial roof measurement options and an existing commercial ESX sample report.

How do I order?

Use the order form on the homepage, select the roof service and ESX format, and submit the property details.

Ready to order a measurement report?

Submit the property address, select your service and file format, and add one or more jobs before payment.
