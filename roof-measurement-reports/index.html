Roof Reports
What is inside a roof measurement report

The exact contents depend on the selected service, but roof reports are designed to give estimators and claims professionals the geometry needed to work from a consistent measurement file.

📏

Linear Measurements

Ridge, hip, valley, rake, eave and other applicable linear measurements.

◩

Roof Area & Faces

Roof-area and facet information, including pitch values where applicable.

📊

Report Tables

PDF reports can include summary tables, waste-factor information and visual roof layouts.
