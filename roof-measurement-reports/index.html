FAQ
What is included in a roof measurement report?

Depending on the service selected, reports can include total roof area, squares, pitch, roof facets, ridges, hips, valleys, rakes, eaves, waste factors, roof sketches, annotations and supporting imagery.

Can I receive both a PDF and an import file?

Yes. You can order a detailed PDF in addition to an ESX or XML file when you need both a visual report and an estimating-platform file.

How quickly are reports delivered?

Our standard service target is 1 business day or less for typical jobs.
