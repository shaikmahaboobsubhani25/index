What a TrueTrace roof measurement report contains

Our deliverables are organized around the measurements estimators and claims professionals use most: roof area, facets, edge lengths, pitches and file formats that can move into estimating software.

PDF report

  • Roof area and facet breakdown
  • Pitch / slope information
  • Ridge, rake, hip, valley and eave lengths
  • Visual roof sketch and aerial imagery
  • Waste-factor table up to 25% where applicable

ESX / XML deliverable

Choose ESX for Xactimate workflows or XML for compatible Symbility / AppliCad workflows. These files are intended to reduce manual re-entry of roof measurements.
