The measurements estimators normally need

A useful roof measurement is more than total square footage. Estimating typically depends on roof area, facet dimensions, pitch and linear edge measurements such as ridge, rake, hip, valley and eave.

Three common measurement methods

1. Field measurement

Direct field measurement can be appropriate when access is safe and the property is nearby, but it requires travel, ladder/roof access and estimator time.

2. Blueprint measurement

For new construction or properties not visible clearly in current imagery, measurements can be built from plans or blueprints.

3. Aerial roof measurement

Aerial measurement uses imagery to trace roof geometry remotely. This can be especially efficient for contractors and adjusters processing multiple properties.

If measuring roofs is taking time away from estimating, sales or claims work, an outsourced aerial measurement service can turn that step into a predictable deliverable.
