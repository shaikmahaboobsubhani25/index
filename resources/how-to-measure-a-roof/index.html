What you need to measure

A complete roof measurement typically involves more than total area. Depending on the roof and the purpose of the estimate, you may need roof area, pitch, eave, ridge, hip, valley and rake lengths, plus individual facet information.

1. Identify the roof footprint and facets

Start by understanding the overall roof shape. Break complex roofs into individual planes or facets so dimensions and pitch can be reviewed separately.

2. Record pitch

Pitch affects true roof surface area and is important when converting plan-view dimensions into sloped roof measurements.

3. Measure key linear features

  • Eaves
  • Ridges
  • Hips
  • Valleys
  • Rakes

4. Calculate roof area and waste

Roof area is commonly converted into roofing squares, with project-specific waste added to account for cuts, overlaps and roof complexity.
